Episode Summary

From losing the company she helped build to 8 figures and then building a seven-figure consulting firm working with 14 female founders and building an iconic personal brand, Gia Smart's story explores Iconic Faith, ambition, taking action, premium identity, and the resilience to rebuild.

The Arc

  • Inherited shame around wealth and ambition
  • Building a decade-plus luxury sales career
  • Company sold before maternity-leave return
  • Doors closed, an Iconic Faith™ rebuild
  • Turning luxury sales expertise into the most elite sales mastery experience for high-level female entrepreneurs — created She Sells Premium™ & The 9-Figure Sales Method

Selected Questions

  • You were taught to dream big and also that wanting significant generational wealth was wrong. How did those messages shape your relationship with ambition?
  • The company you helped build was sold before you returned from maternity leave. What did that loss mean beyond the paycheck?
  • When doors kept closing, what helped you keep trusting God before you could see the next chapter?
  • What did you see women doing in sales that made you create She Sells Premium™?
  • What changes when a woman understands the identity and buyer experience behind a premium sale?
