# Paid-in-Full Masterclass — Conversion Ideas Log

Working log of ideas to test/change for Gia's paid-in-full masterclass. It's not converting yet. Add new entries dated, keep old ones (strike through if tried and rejected).

## 2026-09-12

- **Filter model (Catherine Pruitt model):** Everyone who attends the masterclass is invited to book a session, but Gia doesn't run every session herself — someone else runs the calls and becomes the filter. Mary Julia could be the one running sessions initially.
- **Gifted call in exchange for social proof:** Offer the free call as a "gift" in exchange for a testimonial, a Trustpilot review, or an Instagram like/follow — makes the ask reciprocal instead of one-sided.
- **Paid masterclass instead of free:** Test charging for the masterclass itself rather than giving it away free — changes the qualification of who shows up.
- **Ad/marketing integrity check:** Audit the ad and marketing copy against the actual masterclass content — does it represent what's delivered in deep integrity, and does it promise only what it can truly deliver?
- **Lead-up + follow-up SOP:** Build a clear, automated sequence — email/text lead-up before the masterclass, and a clear follow-up SOP after it — so more of this runs without Gia manually pushing it.
- **Gia-solo masterclass outline:** Now that there's a recording, build a clear outline for a version of the masterclass Gia can run entirely by herself (no co-presenter needed).
- **Immediate follow-up email:** Send a follow-up email right after the masterclass ends, same evening — not the next day.
- **Quote-back email:** Pull quotes from what people said in the chat during the masterclass and email them back individually — thank them for their comment, tell them how much it was loved, that same evening.
- **Text as spam-guard:** Text everyone who attended to make sure the follow-up email doesn't get lost in spam.

## 2026-09-12 (b) — non-converting calls as brand/marketing assets

The 1:1 call is still an asset even when it doesn't close. Applies to three cases:

- **Engaged fangirl, no money to draw from:** Ask permission to record/clip the call — a real "aha" moment from a real prospect beats a scripted testimonial and is raw content for Reels/carousels. Ask for a reaction/testimonial on the spot even without a sale ("just from this hour I already see X"). Put her on a free/low-cost path (waitlist, low-ticket product) instead of losing her, and ask who *she* knows who might be ready now.
- **Loved Gia but already working with someone else:** Not a lost lead — a warm referral source and a comparison-copy source. Ask her directly what made her almost switch / what Gia said that stood out — that language becomes objection-handling and differentiation copy against competitors. Nurture for when her current engagement ends (add a "renewal window" tag/follow-up date) and ask for referrals now since she has no conflict of interest in sending others.
- **Wanted in, but has to "ask her spouse":** Treat as a stalled decision-maker, not a no. Give her something to bring to that conversation — a one-pager, a short recap email of what was discussed, or a case study addressing spousal/financial buy-in objections. Follow up on a specific timeline ("checking back Thursday") rather than leaving it open-ended. If Gia has any spouse-objection talk track/case study already, that's the moment to use it.
- **General principle:** every non-converting call is either content (clips, quotes, objection language) or a nurture-list entry with a reason-coded follow-up (broke / with-competitor / spousal-approval) — none of them should just go cold.
- **No-shows / attended masterclass but never booked:** need a nurture sequence, not a one-shot pitch — recording + mini-training + downsell offer to stay warm for the next cohort/launch instead of disappearing.
