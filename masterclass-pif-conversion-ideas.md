# Paid-in-Full Masterclass — Conversion Ideas Log

Working log of ideas to test/change for Gia's paid-in-full masterclass. It's not converting yet. Add new entries dated, keep old ones (strike through if tried and rejected).

## 2026-09-12

- **Filter model (Catherine Pruitt model):** Everyone who attends the masterclass is invited to book a session, but Gia doesn't run every session herself — someone else runs the calls and becomes the filter. Mary Julia could be the one running sessions initially.
- **Gifted call in exchange for social proof:** Offer the free call as a "gift" in exchange for a testimonial, a Trustpilot review, or an Instagram like/follow — makes the ask reciprocal instead of one-sided.
- **Paid masterclass instead of free:** Test charging for the masterclass itself rather than giving it away free — changes the qualification of who shows up.
- **Ad/marketing integrity check:** Audit the ad and marketing copy against the actual masterclass content — does it represent what's delivered in deep integrity, and does it promise only what it can truly deliver?
- **Lead-up + follow-up SOP:** Build a clear, automated sequence — email/text lead-up before the masterclass, and a clear follow-up SOP after it — so more of this runs without Gia manually pushing it.
- **Gia-solo masterclass outline:** Now that there's a recording, build a clear outline for a version of the masterclass Gia can run entirely by herself (no co-presenter needed).
- **Immediate follow-up email:** Send a follow-up email right after the masterclass ends, same evening — not the next day.
- **Quote-back email:** Pull quotes from what people said in the chat during the masterclass and email them back individually — thank them for their comment, tell them how much it was loved, that same evening.
- **Text as spam-guard:** Text everyone who attended to make sure the follow-up email doesn't get lost in spam.
