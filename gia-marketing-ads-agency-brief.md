## 1. The events being promoted

5 live masterclasses, one per Mastery, run one per week across 5 weeks, in framework order:

| # | Pillar | Working title |
|---|---|---|
| 1. | Iconic Faith  | "Why Premium Buyers Say 'Let Me Think About It'" |
| 2. | Presence Mastery | "The Frequency in the Room" |
| 3. | Communication Mastery | "Stop Selling the Outcome — That's Not What Rich Clients Buy" |
| 4. | Invitation Mastery | "Inviting, Not Convincing" |
| 5. | Enrollment Mastery | "From Alignment to Wire: Mastering the Ask" |

**Registration:** A/B Test: $49 or $97

**Success metrics:** # of registrants, Live attendance per session

**Why now (honest urgency):** each masterclass is a one-time live date, not evergreen. Builds to Core Offer.

## 2. The offer/ Why?

**Purpose:** This series is the launch vehicle for the self-paced course and 4-month Intensive. It should  build authority, reach, and warm followers (email list and IG followers)

**What the ad drives to:**  registration to whichever pillar masterclass is next in the sequence. The promise: Learn to sell to premium buyers: taught live, with the next pillar teased at close.

**Routing:** 5  masterclasses → the self-paced course → 9-Figure Sales Method 4-Month Intensive.

## 3. Order bump / upsell

- [ ] A companion workbook offered during registration before each session
- [ ] All 5 masterclasses as a bundle (and all 5 companion workbooks)
- [ ] The 9-Figure Sales Method self-paced course.

## 4. Objective, KPI, budget

**Primary objective:** registrations per masterclass → live attendance → course enrollment by series close.

**Target metric:** cost per registration per session, plus a single blended CPA to a sale across the full 5-week window

**Budget & flight:** Proposal: test spend on masterclass 1 (Iconic Faith) only, confirm CPA, then scale into 2–5.

**What counts as success:** Set BEFORE flight one launches

## 5. Audience

**Cold:** female founders / coaches / consultants selling high-ticket; premium-positioning and "sales for women" interests; competitor audiences — Leanne Mosley/ Rich Girl, Lindsay Marie / Powerhouse Women, Kathryn Porritt / Icons, Stephanie Ann Swail, Lori Harder, Amy Porterfield, Kelly Roach, Kelly-Reid, etc.

**Warm / retargeting:** each session retargets off the last — masterclass-1 attendees become masterclass-2 registration targets, and so on down the series.

**Exclusions:** current 1:1 clients?

## 6. Creative direction

**Hooks:** one per masterclass, A/B test

**Angles to test:** paradigm-shift, problem-aware, founder-story — same voice rules as the original brief: invest not buy, enroll not close, invite not ask. No income claims, no earnings guarantees, no "results typical."

**Sequencing asset:** a single "5-part masterclass series" landing page or calendar view so cold traffic sees the whole arc, not just one session.

## 7. The funnel it points into

**Destination:** dated registration page per masterclass, on the same platform (GoHighLevel) as the original campaign.

**What receives the lead:** an email sequence + text messages that nurtures across all 5 sessions and pitches the course at series close.

**To confirm:** tracking/UTM set up per session, so each masterclass's CPA is separable.

## 8. Reporting & sync

Weekly, tied to the 5-week flight — report registrations and CPA per session, so an underperforming pillar can be recut before the next one airs.
