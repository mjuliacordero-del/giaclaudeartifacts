What LMD Offers

Three services. Each has its own KPIs.

1. Ads

Job: get leads.

  1. Targets 3 ways:
    • By location: events where your ideal client may attend.
    • By email list: to re-engage your email community.
    • By lead profile: specific demographics based on data crawls.
  2. Targeting gets smarter over time:
    1. Run the first set of ads.
    2. Pull the results from GHL: who was contacted, who didn't qualify, who enrolled.
    3. Run data crawls on those people.
    4. Feed the refined profile back into the ad targeting.
    5. Repeat. Each round has more data.

2. Email nurture

Job: turn leads and subscribers into buyers, and clients into repeat buyers.

3 types of nurture sequences

Each is its own upsell opportunity with its own KPIs.

  1. Leads from ads: the lead gets an email and is enrolled in a sequence that fits what they clicked.
  2. General email list: all general announcements go here, to keep a steady cadence of nurturing.
  3. Current clients: personal touches, like "I read this book this morning and thought of you," or "I'll be at this event. Maybe we'll have dinner together."

3. Automations

Job: the setup in GHL that moves a lead from the ad to the right email and reports back what happened.

  1. Organizes lead data. Every lead is labeled with:
    • The ad it came from, so the matching email sequence fires.
    • Whether it closed, so closes show up without the founder having to report them.
    • Whether it qualified, and why not, which feeds back to the ads.
  2. Runs SMS: sets up SMS consent on every form, then sends texts alongside emails.
  3. Builds workflows: meeting confirmations, event attendee flows, and reminders.
  4. Connects the founder's AI agent to follow-up tasks.
  5. Connects Instagram and email: when the founder posts a reel, the list hears about it (e.g. invited to use her original audio in their own reels).
